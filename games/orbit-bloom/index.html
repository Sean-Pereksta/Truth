Controls and greenhouse guide

Tap a tiled aisle to walk there, or tap a station to walk to its counter and use it. Tap a loose pot to pick it up. Use Set down to leave it on the floor. With a keyboard, move with WASD or arrows, use Space to pick up or work, and Q to set down.

Stand on a marked dock or tap across the gap to ride a platform. To send a pot, tap the gold end of a conveyor while holding it, or stand on that end and press Q. Pick up the pot at the blue end.

Start at a seed tray. Hands-on tasks finish when their bars fill. Set pots down in thermal booths and tend another plant while they grow. Take the plant out when the booth flashes ready, then carry the finished flower to the truck. Use a shelf to hand a plant to another gardener.
