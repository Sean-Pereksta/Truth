Bannerlands - The Lands Update environment atlas

File: lands-atlas.png
Layout: four equal columns by two equal rows, transparent PNG.
Top row: mountain massif; pine cluster; stone bridge; timber bridge.
Bottom row: mountain pass; shallow ford; roadside waymarker; ruined watchtower.

Original artwork generated for this update with the built-in image generation
tool. Prompt: richly hand-painted naturalistic medieval RTS miniature scenery,
isometric 2:1 viewpoint, upper-left daylight, natural stone/moss/wood/water colors,
eight isolated sprites in an exact four-column two-row transparent atlas, no text.
A follow-up edit removed divider lines and colored matte fringes while preserving
the sprites and grid. Runtime extraction suppresses remaining saturated fringe.

The atlas adds scenic landmarks and mountain/woodland depth. Walkable bridge
decks, outside parapets, roads, passes, banks and water ripples are drawn by the
terrain renderer to match the simulation grid precisely. Decorative bridge
sprites never narrow the actual five-lane crossing.

Ground file: lands-ground.png
Opaque material atlas, four equal columns by two equal rows.
Top row: river water; rutted dirt road; stone bridge paving; shallow ford.
Bottom row: rocky pass; mountain slate; grassy riverbank; timber bridge decking.

Ground prompt: full-bleed seamless top-down hand-painted medieval RTS material
swatches, exact 4-by-2 opaque grid, natural subdued water/earth/slate palette,
fine tactile details readable at tile scale, no perspective, labels or borders.
Generated with the built-in image generation tool for this update. The renderer
projects these dedicated material textures onto the isometric ground grid.
