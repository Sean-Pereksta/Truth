CROSSBOWS AND FORMATION ADVANCE

W — ADVANCE IN FORMATION
Select soldiers, choose Line, Wedge or Spread, then press W or click Advance
in formation. They advance together, retain their slots and strike enemies in
reach. The formation turns gradually toward the nearest enemy force of
meaningful size. A lone scout does not turn a large spear line away from a
nearby enemy force. Use a broad Line to bring more spear points into contact.
At narrow crossings, the formation narrows to pass, then restores its ranks.

A — BREAK AND CHARGE
Press A or click Break & charge to release the ranks. Each selected soldier
pursues its own nearest known living enemy troop or building. Cavalry builds
its normal charge with a real run-up. A charge gives up formation protection.
Fog of war still limits target knowledge.

H AND RIGHT MOUSE — HOLD OR WITHDRAW
H holds current positions. Infantry in a held or coordinated formation takes
less damage from the front and resists displacement; flanks and rear remain
exposed. Spear bracing still needs a settled line facing the incoming charge.
Right-click ground without dragging for direct movement and faster withdrawal:
troops can turn toward travel. Right-drag sets a destination and explicit
facing for an orderly march; backing away with weapons forward is slower.
Right-clicking a visible enemy building still focuses attacks on that building.

CROSSBOW CLASSES
Class                Range  Base impact  Cavalry multiplier  Reload mechanism
Gilded Crossbows      5.5        34             1.65          Belt pull
Tollhouse Crossbows   5.8        40             1.75          Foot-stirrup lever
Sharpshooters         7.5        54             1.80          Windlass crank

Range is measured in map cells, before terrain bonuses. Weapon upgrades,
house effects, target defenses and cover still affect final damage. Gilded
Bolts retains its bonus against houses with armor upgrades. Tollhouse crews
retain their faster reload after setting position. Direct bolts obey line of
sight and inflict their damage on impact.

REAL STATIONARY RELOADS
After firing, a crossbowman remains stationary through a mechanical sequence
of lowering the weapon, drawing the string, placing a bolt and raising it.
The three classes use different moving reload mechanisms and timing.
A movement command interrupts the reload immediately. No reload progress is
earned while walking. After arrival, or on the next attack command when
stationary, the crossbowman restarts the full reload before firing again.
Protect the crews with a spear line while they prepare the next volley.
