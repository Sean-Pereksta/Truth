BANNERLANDS - FOG, WATCHES AND BORDER PATROLS

The campaign menu highlights Fog of War with On / Off choices and a visible
explanation. Choose On before starting a new campaign. It is off by default.
Terrain, roads, rivers and crossings remain visible beneath translucent fog.
Unseen enemy armies are hidden. Discovered buildings remain as faded LAST SEEN
images with their observed state; hidden construction, damage and destruction
do not update these memories. Revisit an area to confirm what remains there.

Allied houses share sight. Rivals obey the same knowledge rules: they must scout
enemy armies and structures, and can march to remembered positions without
knowing hidden changes. Enemy army totals, territory shares and private plans
are concealed. Flag locations and alliance scores remain public; local flag
control is only shown inside sight. Castle locations must be discovered.

WATCHTOWERS AND SIGHT
Ordinary infantry and archers see 6 tiles; cavalry and commanders see 8.
Siege troops see 5. Keeps see 9, 10 and 11 tiles by level; ordinary production
buildings see 4. Watchtowers see 12, 15 and 18 tiles by level. Distribute them
through your territory to warn of approaching armies instead of relying only
on the capital garrison. Selecting a tower displays its sight ring.

Rival commanders use different scouting patterns: defensive border screens,
raiding deep probes, objective-focused crossing surveys, cautious surveys for
siege forces, and wide fan sweeps for mass armies. They use cavalry when
available and can send infantry scouts. Watchtower placement favors uncovered
areas, and known threats determine defensive reactions and production.

CAVALRY PATROL
Select cavalry and press N, or click Patrol border. Riders spread along the
reachable edges of their own territory and continue around the perimeter.
Routes refresh as borders move. Riders engage nearby visible enemies, pursue
only within a local leash, then return to their patrol. New Move, Attack, Hold,
Expand or Retreat orders immediately cancel pursuit and patrol. Other troop types in a mixed selection retain their orders.

MOUSE CONTROLS
Hold and drag LEFT: select troops. Shift adds to the selection.
Hold and drag RIGHT: choose destination and facing, regardless of other modes.
Press at the destination, drag toward the desired front, then release.
Right-drag never pans the camera. With no troops selected it gives guidance.
A simple right-click still issues a normal Move, or sets selected buildings'
rally point. Middle-drag, map edges and arrow keys pan the camera.
The D / Move & face guide does not take over left-drag selection.
