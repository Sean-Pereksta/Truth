FORMATIONS, SIGHTLINES AND HOUSE REGIMENTS

NEW HOUSE TROOPS AND BUILDINGS
Stark: Winter Shield Hall       Lannister: Gilded Guard Hall
Targaryen: Dragonshield Foundry  Tyrell: Roseguard Pavilion
These houses can build their own hall (360 gold) to train Shield Bearers.
Two train every 64 seconds before house bonuses. Paid company: 250 gold for 4.
Their heavy shields block 75% of frontal arrows/bolts, 65% of frontal melee
weapon damage and 55% of frontal charge damage. Their flanks and rear are
exposed. They move slowly and deal modest damage.

Mormont: Bear Lance Lodge       Martell: Sun Lance Court
Arryn: Sky Lance Stable
These houses can build their own mounted academy (450 gold) to train one
Lance Champion every 90 seconds before house bonuses. Paid champion: 300 gold.
They have long 1.35-tile lance reach, a strong first charge impact and heavy
knockback, then much weaker hits and a slow 2.3-second weapon attack. Their
charge still requires a real run-up, and braced Frey/Bolton spears counter it.

Each of the seven houses has its own eight-frame troop sprite sheet, armor,
colors and production-building artwork. The buildings unlock paid recruitment,
follow individual rally points and respect supply, placement and cost rules.
Destroying every matching facility locks further paid recruitment.
Rival houses can build and use only their own facilities under the same rules.

River Rangers, Free Folk Berserkers and Ironborn Reavers move 10% faster.

Build the extra house facility with Shift+J, then recruit its troop with
U followed by Shift+6. Each button displays its actual artwork and shortcut.

MOVE AND FACE
Select troops, hold right mouse at the destination, then drag toward the way
the line should face. A line, front arrow and formation slots preview the order.
Release to march there while keeping that facing. The Move & face button (D)
also lets you give the order with a left drag or a touch drag.

Right-click without dragging remains a direct Move order. Middle drag and
screen edges pan the camera. Right drag pans when no troops are selected.
Escape or a canceled gesture clears the preview without issuing an order.

Facing marches do not chase enemies. Backward movement uses 55% of forward
speed, sideways movement uses 80%, and troops turn gradually to their facing.
When a Move reaches its position it becomes Hold and preserves the facing.
H holds the line at its current positions. A new ordinary movement or attack
order releases the explicit facing. Cavalry cannot charge while backpedaling.

FREY SPEARS AND BOLTON PIKES
Bridge Wardens and Dread Pikes remain their houses' specialists. Their normal
house production buildings and recruitment requirements are unchanged.
Face them toward an approaching charge, settle the line and wait about half
a second for their brace. A living, braced spear stops a frontal rider and
retaliates. They hit cavalry harder than ordinary swordsmen; pikes have the
stronger anti-cavalry attack, while the Frey shield line offers better frontal
protection. Moving, flanked or rear-attacked spears cannot stop a charge.

Held or explicitly facing infantry in Line formation reduces frontal damage.
Melee attacks from the rear deal 30% extra damage, or 12% from the side.
Facing and protection rules apply to player and rival soldiers alike.

CAVALRY FLANKS
A real straight run-up is still required. Charge impacts from the rear deal
45% more damage and spend 55% less collision momentum; side impacts gain 15%
damage and spend 25% less momentum. Braced spears still stop frontal charges.
Terrain, physical contact and the existing charge hit limits still apply.

SIGHTLINES AND TERRAIN
Mountains block direct arrows and bolts, including keep and watchtower fire.
Mountain passes remain open. An exact mountain corner cannot be used as a
firing slit. Archers choose visible targets; blocked direct shots do no damage.
Troops in forests take 25% less damage from arrows and bolts. Archers firing
from hills gain 0.6 tiles of range. Arcing catapult rocks pass over mountains
and ignore woodland cover. Player and enemy armies use the same rules.

The sightline index refreshes when terrain changes. Open ground uses a fast
terrain lookup; difficult rays have a fixed cache limit. Facing previews are
capped, and extra facing marks are drawn only for selected troops.

SELECT TROOP TYPES
Alt+I selects infantry, including the house's infantry specialists.
Alt+S selects Frey spears or Bolton pikes when present in your army.
Alt+A selects archers. Alt+C selects cavalry. Alt+G selects siege engines.
Alt+H selects commanders. Hold Shift as well to add a type to your selection.
Alt+B selects Shield Bearers; Alt+L selects Lance Champions.
The Army selection buttons offer the same actions; Shift-click adds troops.

COMMAND GROUPS
Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+9 saves the selected soldiers to a group.
1 through 9 recalls it. Ctrl+Shift+1 through 9 adds selected soldiers to it.
Saving an empty selection clears the group. Fallen soldiers are skipped.
Group buttons show living counts: click recalls; Ctrl-click saves;
Ctrl+Shift-click adds. Groups last for the current campaign and do not change
troop orders or recruitment rally points merely by saving or selecting them.

BUILD WITH LETTERS
Shift+B: Barracks         Shift+A: Archery range
Shift+S: Stables          Shift+T: Watchtower
Shift+W: Siege works      Shift+H: House production building
Shift+J: New shield/champion facility, for the seven eligible houses
These shortcuts work from any battlefield panel. Click owned ground to build.
Esc or right-click cancels. Shift while placing keeps the building tool active.
B opens the Build panel. Building buttons show their letter shortcuts.
Muster uses Shift+1 through 5 and Shift+6 for an eligible new house regiment;
Council uses Shift+1 through 6.

The compact campaign menu is included in this playable copy as well.
