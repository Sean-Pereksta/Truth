BANNERLANDS - RUINS AND REINFORCEMENTS

CASTLE REALMS
Standard: 3 neutral ruins. Epic: 5. Continent: 6.
Starting castles are additional to these neutral ruins.

Hold at least 90% of living troops inside a ruin's four-tile capture ring for
60 seconds. The same alliance dominance rules as flags apply. Empty or contested
time pauses each alliance's saved progress. The house with the strongest local
presence receives the castle when its alliance completes the capture.

Every house begins with 280 troop capacity and its opening castle. Each
additional held castle adds 60 capacity: one castle allows 280 troops, two allow
340, three allow 400. Owned land, completed Bannerlands, production buildings
and upgrades do not increase troop capacity. Territory still supplies income
and structure slots. Restoring a castle immediately claims passable tiles
within an eight-tile radius, preserving allied territory. Restored castles
train and defend like keeps.

A destroyed castle becomes a neutral ruin and can be captured again. Losing an
additional castle removes its 60 capacity immediately; existing excess troops
survive, but further recruitment waits for supply. Losing the opening capital
moves home to another surviving castle. Your faction falls only when its last
castle is destroyed.
Flag and Dominion victory conditions still apply in their respective modes.

ADDITIONAL HOUSE REGIMENTS
Build each facility with Shift+J. Muster its regiment with U, then Shift+6.
Each facility must survive to unlock paid recruitment. Supply, gold and rally
rules remain in force.

Bolton: Dreadfort Siege Yard. Hightower: Hightower Siege Yard.
Both train trebuchets, one every 85 seconds before house modifiers. Trebuchets
have 10.5-tile range, powerful arcing splash bombardment and a 7.5-second reload.
They need 15 stationary seconds to deploy; moving or being knocked aside resets
setup. The battlefield shows setup progress and the deployed machine.

Free Folk: Giant Camp.
Giants have 1,250 base health. Each club hit damages and pushes back the main
target and nearby enemy troops. Heavier units, frontal shields and knockback
resistance reduce the shove. Allies and buildings are never pushed, and terrain
blocks displacement.
Each camp musters one giant per 130 seconds. Paid and automatic mustering share
the same camp cycle. Paid companies contain exactly one giant. The recruitment
card shows the remaining wait.

Tully: Sharpshooter Lodge.
Sharpshooters fire at 6.25 tiles, farther than ordinary archers, but reload for
3.4 seconds. They still need direct sightlines. The lodge trains two every
48 seconds before house modifiers.

CHAMPION BALANCE AND REGROUPING
Lance Champions have 400 base health and 18 base damage. The first charge
remains their main weapon; normal attacks still take 2.3 seconds. Move orders
gain 30% speed, faster turns and less crowd drag. Crowds still slow champions, but they escape more easily than ordinary cavalry. Retreat V sends them
to their training facility’s rally, then army rally or home as a fallback.
Rival champions disengage after successful charges and regroup for another run.

RIVAL STRATEGY AND ARTWORK
Armies treat ruins as major objectives, assign capture companies, hold the ring,
defend allied castles and convert successful castle sieges into capture missions.
Their production plans include the new faction facilities.

The castle states, three eight-frame troop sheets and four production buildings
are generated transparent PNG artwork. The source assets and exact prompts are
in assets/conquest. All are embedded in the standalone Bannerlands.html file.
