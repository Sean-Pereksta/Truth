TROOP IDENTITY, EQUIPMENT AND FORMATIONS

This guide describes the coordinated equipment update. Numerical combat values
are initial tuning targets. The older update notes describe historical releases.

TROOPS AND RECRUITMENT
Stark's Winter Pike Hall trains Winter Pikemen; Tyrell's Thornwall Drill Hall
trains Thornwall Pikemen. These two-handed pike regiments have no shield
protection. Winter Guard remains a separate shield-equipped Stark specialist.
Lannister and Targaryen retain their heavy Shield Bearers. Build the extra
regiment facility with Shift+J and recruit with Muster, Shift+6.

Legacy Stark/Tyrell Shield Bearer records convert in place to their pike
regiments. The migration preserves IDs, orders, group membership, upgrades and
remaining-health percentage. Legacy facility names map to the corresponding
pike hall. The game has no disk save/load interface; migrateRegimentState is the
snapshot migration entry point, and Game.migrateLegacyRegiments updates a live
simulation and its indexes.

River Rangers carry compact bows, travel cloaks and satchels. Sharpshooters use
heavy windlass crossbows and bolt cases. Hill Huntsmen have peaked hunting caps,
mantles and longbows. Tollhouse crews use broad flat helmets and stirrup-lever
crossbows. Horn Hill Halberdiers carry complete axe-hook-point polearms. Vale
Sentinels use a low marksman stance and compact lever crossbows.

COMPLETE TROOP FRAMES
The complete-troops module authors whole figures with their weapons and hands
in the same frame. Shared cached frames retain full weapons at every detail
level. Ground selection guides remain separate from troop artwork. Projectiles
become world objects only on release. New pikes and foot polearms have sixteen
directions; mounted lances, crossbows and other affected troops have eight.
The pose metadata in equipment.js supplies grip, weapon tip, release point and
protective direction to rendering and combat.

Alt+S               Select foot spears, pikes and halberdiers

Form Up shows every projected soldier slot, blocked ground, front and handles.
Move its center, drag either side for width, and turn the front handle to face.
Ranks range from one to four; width and rank controls update together. Enter
places and Escape cancels. Pike Block and Shield Wall settle into Hold/Brace
after arrival. Lock Facing preserves the commanded front. Group assignment
remembers the preferred preset, dimensions and facing lock.

Touch selection exposes Form, Hold/Brace, Advance and Retreat above the compact
dock. Form first opens preset/rank controls, then battlefield placement with
position, width and facing handles plus Place/Cancel. Ordinary touch drag pans
and pinch zooms outside placement. Orders retains Face and Charge.

FORMATION AND POLEARM READINESS
Soldiers retain stable slot assignments. A nearby compatible rear soldier can
fill a casualty gap without rebuilding every neighbor's stance. Advancing
cohorts compress at narrow passages and reopen afterward. Separated members
catch up without stopping the main body indefinitely. Ranged soldiers occupy
rear ranks rather than being inserted into the polearm front.

Moving, Forming, Turning, Lowering, Braced and Disrupted are distinct states.
Full brace requires a valid slot, aligned weapon and about 0.5 seconds of
settling. Movement, turning and displacement remove full readiness. The visible
weapon can still offer reduced resistance while presented during an advance or
backward march. Ordinary Move uses a travel carry.

Interception checks the rider's swept movement against the actual forward
weapon contact zone before body impact. It requires a living soldier, a clear
lane and a presented point. The central planted sector is +/-45 degrees.
Initial momentum losses are 90% for a planted heavy pike, 80% for a heavy spear,
50-60% for lighter spears/halberds and 30% for aligned moving contact. A rear
pass or a missed point has no interception effect. Contact events are discrete,
so remaining against one shaft does not deal a free hit each simulation frame.

Ready, aim, release, lower, span, take bolt, seat bolt, raise, ready are tied to
simulation work. Each loaded bolt releases one projectile; damage belongs to
impact. The shared crossbow path applies cavalry vulnerability once. Gilded
crews span with a belt hook, Tollhouse crews with a stirrup/lever, Sharpshooters
with a windlass, and Vale crews with their compact lever.

Loaded weapons remain loaded in transit. Unloaded weapons cannot complete a
loading stage while walking. Move/Retreat interrupt immediately. Completed
physical stages survive, progress inside the interrupted stage is discarded,
and stationary crews resume the next unfinished stage. Minor collision shuffles
do not repeatedly restart loading. The selected panel reports loading state.

SHIELD FACING
Heavy shields receive 25% of frontal missile damage, 35% of frontal melee and
45% of frontal charge damage. Their sides receive 100%; rear direct hits 150%.
Small carried shields receive 60%, 75%, 80%, 100% and 125%, respectively.
The front and rear sectors each span 120 degrees. Frontal protection requires
an actual raised protective pose. Rear exposure remains during travel/turning.

Missiles use their incoming travel direction at impact. Charges use the horse's
approach and melee uses the attacker/contact direction. One directional
modifier applies per direct hit; shield rear vulnerability replaces older rear
bonuses. Poison, fire and area damage follow their own rules.

VALIDATION

npm run test:mobile and npm run test:standalone. Native raster checks require
