THE LANDS UPDATE
Choose Crownlands, Riverlands, Highland Marches, Shattered Coast or Kingsroad
in the campaign menu. Open Realm preserves the original open battlefield.
Painted river, road, bridge, ford, pass and mountain textures match terrain that
changes actual travel. Five-lane stone bridges have broad bank approaches;
water and solid mountains block troops outside crossings and passes.
Player and rival armies share weighted routes and terrain movement costs.
Lords consider route length, road access and defended crossings when invading.
See Lands-Update.txt for landscape details and movement rules.

ADAPTIVE BOT PRODUCTION
Bots plan their production network as part of their invasion strategy. They compare
barracks, archery ranges, stables, siege works, house specialist buildings and defenses
instead of following a fixed building rotation. Army shortages, enemy troop types,
existing training speed, income and territory influence what they choose next.
They can build multiple useful facilities of the same type, replace destroyed ones,
and grow larger networks when the economy and army demand support them.
Bots save for a chosen building, place it on safe owned ground, and use the same
gold costs, land-based structure limit and army supply rules as the player.
Near full army supply they stop adding unnecessary training capacity. During capital
emergencies they prioritize defenders. Production is no longer held behind two town
hall upgrades or the old fifteen-building ceiling.
The house roster shows what each bot is building or saving for; hover for its reason.

MOUSE CAMERA MOVEMENT
Move the mouse to any battlefield edge to pan the camera. Corners pan diagonally.
Move toward the center or onto the sidebar to stop. The closer you are to the edge,
the faster the view moves. This also works while paused. Right/middle dragging and
arrow keys remain available. Edge scrolling stops during drags, menus and dialogs.

DRAWN ARTWORK AND UPGRADEABLE WATCHTOWERS
This package includes nine new painted sprites: three distinct capital levels,
three distinct watchtower levels, and neutral, battle and royal capture flags.
Buildings use their own level artwork on the battlefield and in their portraits.
Blue cloth and roofs take your faction/team colors. All artwork is embedded in
the offline game and also included as a transparent PNG atlas in Source/dist/assets.
The generation prompt is included beside the atlas in campaign-art-prompt.txt.

Click one of YOUR watchtowers to select it, then use Upgrade tower in the sidebar.
Level 1 Watchtower:    1,150 health, 25 base damage, 4.6 range.
Level 2 Guard Tower:  2,000 health, 38 base damage, 5.2 range; costs 250 gold.
Level 3 Royal Bastion:3,200 health, 55 base damage, 5.9 range; costs 450 gold.
Upgrades preserve the tower's percentage of health remaining. Weapons upgrades
and House Arryn's Falcon Guard combine with the tower's improved values.
Bots also upgrade their watchtowers using the same prices and rules.

BANNERLANDS - STRATEGIES, STANDARDS AND ALLIANCES

Open Bannerlands.html to play offline. Start a new campaign to use the new rules.

SIX GAME MODES
Capital Conquest: destroy every rival capital.
Bannerfall: first side to capture 5 standards wins; destroying all rival capitals also wins.
Dominion: hold 65% of the realm after 90 seconds, or destroy all rival capitals.
Each mode has a team version. Choose 2v2, 3 paired teams, up to 8 paired teams.
You have one allied bot; opposing houses are paired into their own alliances.
Allies share a main team color, with lighter shades identifying individual houses.
Allies do not damage each other or steal one another's land. Their forces support
each other's territorial captures, flag control and capital defense.

TOWN HALLS - YOUR CAPITAL IS YOUR LIFELINE
Level 1 Town Hall:       2,500 health, 24 base damage, 5.5 range.
Level 2 Fortified Keep:  4,500 health, 36 base damage, 6.2 range; costs 500 gold.
Level 3 Royal Citadel:   7,500 health, 52 base damage, 7.0 range; costs 950 gold.
Open Council and use Upgrade Town Hall. The portrait and battlefield artwork grow
through three levels, adding fortified walls, towers and animated banners.
Upgrades preserve the percentage of health remaining. Ordinary weapons upgrades
also improve capital firepower. Bots buy the same upgrades with their own gold.
When a capital is destroyed, its entire faction's troops, buildings and territory
are wiped immediately. If YOUR capital falls, you lose even if your ally survives.
Protect it and answer allied calls for defense.

CAPTURE STANDARDS - AVAILABLE IN EVERY MODE
There are three physical flags on the battlefield. Click an objective in the
Standards panel to locate it. Its highlighted area has a radius of 4 map cells.
Have at least 90% of all living troops inside that area to advance your hold timer.
In a team mode, both allied armies count together. Exactly 90% is sufficient.
Hold for 30 accumulated seconds to secure the standard. Contested or empty time
FREEZES your progress; it never erases it. A rival tracks its own saved time.
Each successful capture awards eight Royal Guard specialist troops at your capital:
50% extra health and 35% extra weapon damage, plus each house's specialist effects.
Allied houses split the eight troops. The reward arrives even at full supply;
ordinary recruitment waits until there is space again. Royal Guard display gold stars.
The captured flag rearms for 20 seconds. The winner's hold timer resets; other
sides retain their saved time. Bannerfall counts captures toward a five-flag win.
In Conquest and Dominion, captures provide reinforcements without ending the game.

STRATEGIC RIVALS
Five distinct plans appear in the house roster:
Grand host: gather a large force and invade as one army.
Flanking raiders: fast, smaller waves take side routes toward exposed production.
Siege column: gather artillery and escorts to break defended capitals.
Bastion & counterattack: stronger home guard, fortifications and patient counterattacks.
Banner guard: prioritize standards and hold their capture areas.
Bots weigh every hostile house equally, including other computer-controlled lords.
Target location includes the march from home, distance from owned frontiers,
shared borders, and exposure to other hostile lands and forts along the approach.
Guards, towers, damaged buildings and territory still matter. Retaliation has a
limited influence, and independent rivals prefer to open another viable front
when a house is already under invasion. Allied houses can still coordinate attacks.
Equal target scores use a stable seeded choice without favoring the player.
Bots recruit for their plan and counter nearby hostile troop types. They muster at home,
keep reserves, retreat wounded soldiers and regroup between committed waves.
Small scouting parties expand separately; new recruits do not trickle into an invasion.

COORDINATED TEAM ORDERS
In a team campaign, use Call assault or Call defense, then click the battlefield.
Assault: bots gather for 8 seconds before launching toward the chosen position.
Defense: allied bots respond immediately to protect the selected position.
Calls appear on the battlefield, minimap and Alliance Orders panel.
An assault call lasts up to 100 seconds; a defense call lasts 45 seconds.
Bots also request help for threatened allied capitals and call joint offensives.
They retain home guards and prioritize emergencies at their own capital.
Your calls direct allied bots; your own soldiers continue to follow your orders.
Esc or right-click cancels placing a call.

Existing troop selection, building rally points, formations, house powers,
specialist production, expansion and 500-level upgrades remain available.
