TROOP ORDERS AND LARGE-ARMY PERFORMANCE

Controls
- A right-click over the battlefield now closes an open command drawer and
  issues the selected army's Move order in the same click.
- A right-drag that returns to its starting point issues an ordinary Move.
  Directional drags still set the destination and facing at the pressed point.
- Primary and touch taps keep their existing drawer-dismissal behavior.

Routing
- Once the shared route-search budget is spent, new destinations wait without
  allocating full-map flow fields. This preserves unfinished routes instead
  of evicting them under a stream of commands and leaving troops frozen.
- Cached terrain regions reject disconnected destinations immediately.
  Rivers and mountains remain impassable except at legal crossings.

Performance
- The collision grid clears only previously occupied buckets. Candidate order,
  physical separation, combat results and troop speeds are preserved.
- Unchanged territory reuses ownership pixels, border geometry and minimap
  artwork. Changed borders avoid temporary per-tile arrays. Fog memory,
  passability, faction colors and new campaigns invalidate the cache.

Validation
- The main test suite includes repeated orders for 320 mixed troops, complete
  bridge crossings and reversal, 480 selected troops receiving a right-click,
  deferred route contention, sparse collision queries and territory caching.
- Real Chrome input checks cover 480 selected troops, an open command drawer,
  twelve repeated right-clicks, returning drags and directional drags.
- The full gameplay and native raster rendering suites pass.
- A seeded continent simulation benchmark with 1,120 starting troops and
  16 houses used 40 warm-up updates and 160 measured updates per scenario.
  Idle, march and battle state digests matched the previous version exactly.
  Two local runs reduced mean update time by 42-56% for idle armies, 30-48%
  for marching armies and 20-31% for battles. Both runs retained matching state.
  These are simulation CPU measurements, not whole-game FPS guarantees.
- The ownership-generation benchmark on 39,424 tiles reduced unchanged
  refreshes from 15.30 to 1.14 ms and changing refreshes from 15.67 to 3.00 ms.
  This targeted measurement excludes the canvas upload and other rendering.
