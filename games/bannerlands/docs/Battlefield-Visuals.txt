BANNERLANDS — BATTLEFIELD VISUALS

ATTACK WEIGHT AND CONTACT
Weapon contact frames now coincide with the simulation's actual damage or
projectile release. Hammer windups lead into the downward contact pose and
recovery, long halberds show a thrust and retraction, and crossbows visibly
cock and reload over their actual recruitment-independent attack interval.
Struck troops briefly recoil; frontal defensive blocks produce compact sparks.
Stormbreaker knockback remains physical and still respects terrain and resistance.
Frey Bridge Wardens render about 27% larger so their extended polearms do not
shrink the bearers. Every walk/attack frame and both facings retain the full
weapon, shield and feet; other houses keep their established display sizes.
These presentation changes do not delay attacks or alter damage, health,
cooldowns, range, collision rules or house balance.

TERRAIN
Worn, uneven road shoulders and wheel impressions blend roads into grass.
Forest boundaries gain undergrowth; land-side shorelines gain banks and reeds.
Water, mountains, bridges, fords and passes retain their gameplay boundaries.
The grid is faint during ordinary play and becomes clearer when building or
previewing a formation facing order. G still toggles the ordinary grid.
Terrain accents are baked into the existing overview and terrain chunk caches;
they are not regenerated every frame.

FORMATION CRESTS
Nearby formations of at least six visible soldiers receive one small floating
house crest. These are the exact painted shields in house selection, sampled
from assets/house-emblems.png. No cloth flag or substitute symbol is used.
The crest stays 17 screen pixels wide, above the formation's tallest soldiers.
Isolated scouts receive no marker. Limits prevent clutter in large battles.
Hidden troops cannot contribute to a crest or its position; fog loss removes
the marker immediately, including while paused.

BRIDGE GRAPHICS
Bridge sidewalls use generated painted masonry, alpha-transparent arch spans
and matching terminal/corner posts from assets/bridges/stone-edges.png.
Pieces follow both isometric bridge directions and join at actual endpoints.
The left/front rail overlaps its rear/start post and the deck; the deck covers
the right/back wall and post bases. The near terminal post draws last.
The walkable deck and all crossing/route rules remain unchanged.
Graphics are baked into bounded bridge scenery caches.

PERFORMANCE
Idle and marching troops retain a direct cached-sprite drawing path. Distant
views skip imperceptible cosmetic body poses. Auto detail retains contact frames
and impact cues while reducing unselected body transforms and weapon accents.
Formation linking refreshes at 10Hz; moving centroids and current visibility
are still checked every rendered frame. Only on-screen troops are considered.
The roster reuses one visible-army scan instead of repeating it for every house.
The house crest atlas is decoded and cropped once; markers draw cached images.

VALIDATION
Run npm run check and npm test for syntax and gameplay regressions.
Run npm run test:render with LANDS_CANVAS_MODULE for native raster checks.
Run npm run test:visual-performance with Playwright and BANNERLANDS_BROWSER
for repeatable browser comparisons against the previous main renderer.
Initial and optimized measurements are environment-specific, not a promise
of a fixed frame rate on every machine or at every army size.
