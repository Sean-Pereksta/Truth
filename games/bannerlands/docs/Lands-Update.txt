THE LANDS UPDATE

The realm now has geography that changes the war. Select a landscape when
starting a campaign. Every landscape works with all three sizes, all six victory
modes, and up to sixteen houses. The same seed reproduces the same terrain.

FIVE NEW LANDSCAPES

Crownlands: rolling woodland, a winding river, and royal roads linked by stone
crossings. A balanced landscape for learning how roads and bridges shape a front.

Riverlands: twin rivers and a broad tributary divide the battlefield. Multiple
bridges and shallow fords offer different approaches across the waterways.

Highland Marches: mountain walls divide wooded valleys. Broad passes become
defensible invasion routes; an open-looking destination can require a long march.

Shattered Coast: tidal inlets create peninsulas and coastal approaches. Causeways
and roads connect the land, while water protects otherwise exposed flanks.

Kingsroad: deep woods surround a dense road network and a central river. Roads
favor mobile forces, while forest detours are expensive for cavalry and artillery.

Open Realm preserves the original battlefield without interior water or mountain
barriers. It remains available for open-field battles.

BRIDGES, PASSES AND MOVEMENT

Water and solid mountains block every troop type. Bridges and fords cross water;
passes cross mountains. All sides use the same movement and routing rules.

Designed bridges have five actual walkable lanes and wide approaches. The stone
deck covers those lanes, with parapets on the outside. Companies retain separate
lanes instead of being forced through one point. Mountain passes are also broad.
Ordinary roads are three tiles wide; the Kingsroad has broader main roads.

Roads take 0.78 times ordinary grass travel time (about 28% faster movement), and
bridge decks take 0.9 times grass travel time. Woods, slopes, passes and shallow
fords take longer, particularly for cavalry and siege troops. Route choices and
movement speed use these same costs. House movement bonuses still apply.

Troops find routes around rivers and mountain walls, use suitable crossings, and
reform near the destination. Move orders, attack orders, expansion parties, rally
points, retreats, and enemy armies share the routing system. Charges, knockback
and crowd separation cannot push troops through blocked terrain. Troops continue
marching across bridges and passes while claiming land rather than stopping on
the deck to capture every tile.

Buildings need clear ground. Roads, bridges, fords and passes remain open for
traffic. A building footprint cannot straddle a riverbank or mountain wall.
Crossings can be claimed as territory and defended by troops or nearby towers.

LORD STRATEGY

Lords compare reachable routes and travel costs when selecting a rival. A nearby
capital across a mountain wall can be a worse target than a farther capital on
an open road. Defenders at bridges, fords and passes add danger to an approach.
Fortifications and other hostile territory are checked along the actual route.
Raiders evaluate cavalry travel costs; siege columns evaluate artillery costs.
Invasion and flanking timers allow for the length and difficulty of the march.
Standards are also chosen using route cost rather than straight-line distance.

Safe starting reservations remain equally clear for every house, with the same
starting army, gold and owned land. Generated maps connect usable land and
starting positions, and standards are placed on reachable ground.

READING THE LAND

The campaign selector includes a generated preview and a description of each
landscape. The battlefield and minimap show roads, mountains, water and crossings.
Hover over terrain to read its access and movement effects. The terrain legend
explains the map colors. Mountain, woodland, ford and roadside artwork accompany
riverbanks, animated river ripples, continuous bridge decks and road tracks.

VALIDATION

Run npm run check and npm test from the game directory. Tests cover generated
map connectivity, starting safety, bridge widths, weighted routes, collision,
formation traffic, enemy target decisions, and the existing combat/economy rules.
Build an offline edition with node source/build-standalone.mjs <output-directory>.
