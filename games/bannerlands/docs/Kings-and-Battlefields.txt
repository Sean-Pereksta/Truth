BANNERLANDS - KINGS, STANDARDS AND BATTLEFIELDS

THE BATTLEFIELD AFTER A FIGHT
Fallen soldiers leave faction-colored bodies with several poses and clothing
shades. Riders leave fallen mounts, and siege troops leave wreckage. Bodies
fade continuously over two minutes of unpaused game time, then disappear.
Fading continues on the victory screen. Bodies never block movement, attack,
capture land or flags, provide sight, or appear in troop selections or totals.
Fog hides bodies outside current allied sight. Very large battles use bounded
cosmetic storage so old casualties cannot accumulate indefinitely.

DEFEND THE KING
Choose Defend the King in the campaign menu. The battle always has four armies,
each from a different house, and one crowned king per army. The king has one
life: killing him immediately eliminates his army, surviving buildings and
projectiles. The last king standing wins. Your king's death ends your campaign.
Losing a castle does not eliminate an army while its king survives.

The first three reinforcement flags appear in the central battlefield after
15 seconds. A fresh wave appears every 45 seconds, replacing unclaimed flags.
Hold at least 90% of the troops in a flag's ring for 12 seconds to claim it.
Contested or empty rings pause progress until the next wave replaces the flag.
Each captured flag disappears and awards 12 mixed infantry, archers and cavalry
beside your living king. These reinforcements need no castle or free supply.
Ordinary paid and automatic recruitment remain available through facilities.

Kings wear crowns, have gold health bars and a KING label, and have distinct
minimap markers. Find king focuses your king. The king panel shows your health;
enemy health is only available while the enemy king is currently visible.
Enemy commanders retain a royal guard, send companies to capture standards,
withdraw injured kings, and hunt observed rival kings. Fog still limits their
knowledge, targeting and scouting.

CROWN CONTROL
One central Crown Standard determines victory. Accumulate 120 seconds of
uncontested control with at least 90% troop dominance in its ring. Empty or
contested time pauses each army's saved total; another army taking control
does not erase it. Destroying every rival castle also wins.

BLITZ FLAGS
A faster flag campaign: capture a standard in 10 seconds and reach three
captures to win. Captures award eight elite Royal Guard; each flag reopens
after 12 seconds. Destroying every rival castle also wins.

THREE NEW LANDSCAPES
Twin Lakes: two large lakes with causeways and flanking approaches around an
open central battlefield.
Mountain Crown: a mountain ring and four radial gates surround the central
arena; passes and roads give each side several routes.
Four Rivers: four river arms and stone bridges divide the outer approaches
while leaving an open central battlefield for flags and army clashes.

All nine landscapes work with existing modes, map sizes, faction choices and
optional fog. Starting armies have clear deployment ground and land routes
remain connected. Castle modes retain sparse, capturable ruins.
