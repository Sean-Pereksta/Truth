BANNERLANDS — HOUSE REINFORCEMENTS

Six new troops have separate painted 4-column by 2-row animation sheets:
four walk frames and four attack frames. Six matching buildings are in a
transparent 3-column by 2-row atlas. Artwork and prompts: assets/reinforcements.
The standalone build embeds all of them. Start a new campaign for this roster.

BARATHEON — STORMBREAKERS / STORMBREAKER FORGE
Forge: 430 gold; trains 2 every 48 seconds. Paid company: 290 gold for 3.
Heavy hammer infantry: 235 health, 36 damage, 2.1-second swings, 0.85 reach.
Each blow produces a gold impact ring, sparks and dust, and physically shoves
surviving enemies. Heavy troops, frontal greatshields and braced spears resist
the shove. Braced spears take 25% extra hammer damage. Mountains and blocked
terrain constrain knockback; buildings are never pushed. Slow movement and
swings expose them to missiles. Baratheon's 15% cavalry surcharge remains.

GREYJOY — SALTWIND AXETHROWERS / SALTWIND LODGE
Lodge: 350 gold; trains 3 every 36 seconds. Paid company: 230 gold for 4.
108 health, 29 damage, 2-second throws, 2.65 range. Visible spinning axe
projectiles give raiders short-range support. Ordinary archers outrange them.
Light armor and slow follow-up volleys punish prolonged exchanges.
Greyjoy still recovers 50% more slowly on owned ground.

FREY — TOLLHOUSE CROSSBOWS / TOLLHOUSE RANGE
Range: 380 gold; trains 3 every 42 seconds. Paid company: 250 gold for 4.
125 health, 28 damage, 4.25 range, 2.3-second base reload. After 2 seconds
stationary, reloads are 25% faster. Movement and knockback reset readiness.
Visible bolts obey sightlines. Vulnerable to flanks and arcing siege fire.
Frey's 20% cavalry surcharge remains. Paid crossbows require a living
Tollhouse Range; split companies use matching ranges when two survive.

REED — CRANNOG SPEARHANDS / CRANNOG SPEAR LODGE
Lodge: 330 gold; trains 3 every 34 seconds. Paid company: 220 gold for 5.
142 health, 19 damage, 1.15 reach. Spears deal twice base damage to cavalry.
They receive an extra 30% forest movement bonus on top of Reed's 20% house
bonus, for a combined 1.56 movement multiplier before terrain costs.
They have no charge-stopping brace and remain lightly armored.
Reed's 20% slower automatic catapult training remains.

TARLY — HORN HILL HALBERDIERS / HORN HILL DRILL HALL
Hall: 390 gold; trains 3 every 42 seconds. Paid company: 260 gold for 4.
188 health, 24 damage, 1.55-second attacks, 1.6-tile reach. Longer painted
halberds and actual attack range let them hit before ordinary swordsmen close.
Halberds deal twice base damage to cavalry but cannot hard-stop a charge.
Slow movement and exposed flanks preserve the importance of disciplined lines.
Tarly's slower automatic archer recruitment remains.

MARTELL — SUN HORSE ARCHERS / SUNBOW CORRAL
Corral: 430 gold; trains 2 every 46 seconds. Paid company: 300 gold for 3.
132 health, 17 damage, 3.8 range, 1.65-second shots, 2.35 base riding speed.
Mounted movement, arrow projectiles, Viper Venom and Attack-order skirmishing.
They cannot initiate impact charges. Light armor and half damage to buildings
leave them vulnerable to focused missiles and poor at breaking fortifications.
Catapults and siege works are removed from Martell's menus and rejected by
recruitment, construction, training and AI. The opening catapult becomes one
horse archer, keeping the equal 70-soldier start. Lance Champions remain.
Martell still pays 15% extra for paid infantry.

CONTROLS AND SHARED RULES
For the five newly expanded houses, Shift+J builds the additional facility;
Muster Shift+6 recruits its company. Martell uses its former catapult slot:
Muster Shift+4 recruits horse archers, Shift+W builds the Sunbow Corral
(Shift+K is an alternative). Shift+J and Shift+6 retain Lance Champions.
Alt+S includes Reed spears and Tarly halberds alongside Frey/Bolton spears.
Horse archers are cavalry for Alt+C and border patrol. Their selected power
button can activate Viper Venom; the Charge passive is not shown for them.
All recruits obey supply, gold, surviving exact facility and local rally rules.
Troop capacity starts at 280 with one castle. Each additional held castle adds
60: two castles allow 340 troops, three allow 400. Land, completed Bannerlands,
production buildings and upgrades do not increase troop capacity.
Player and enemy houses use the same roster and effects. Existing house
weaknesses remain; these are initial balance values subject to playtesting.
