      THE COMMANDER’S HANDBOOK

      Win the war, one tile at a time.

      Troops claim ground around them. Outnumber rivals locally to capture contested ground. Own more ground to earn gold and increase army supply. Structure capacity is 16 base slots plus 1 per 8 owned land tiles, including your keep, with no fixed ceiling. The Build panel shows your current total. Losing land preserves existing structures but can block new construction. Automatic training now takes twice as long, and troops have about 25% more health for longer battles.

      Set your rally points

      Click your building to select it, then right-click a land destination or use Set rally point. New troops from that building march there. Press R to select all your buildings and set one shared destination. Clear rally keeps new troops beside the selected buildings. Troop movement orders never change rally points. Selected troops show E for an available house ability; passive specialist effects are labeled Automatic.

      The Lands Update

      Choose a landscape before raising your banners. Deep rivers and mountain peaks block movement; armies find bridges, shallow fords and mountain passes. Roads speed marching, while fords, hills and forests can slow different troops. Hover the ground for terrain guidance. Lords weigh real routes and defended crossings when choosing invasions. Crossings remain open to every house; controlling their approaches wins battles.

      Keep expanding

      Select troops and press T, or click Expand nearby. They repeatedly secure nearby land connected to your territory, choosing new tiles automatically. If there is no frontier to capture, they wait and retry. A new movement or battle order replaces expansion. If any member of the expansion party is attacked, the party stops expanding and holds to defend; press T again to resume. The order can be given while paused.

      A slower economy and deeper upgrades

      Conquering land raises income gradually; large empires gain less from each additional tile. Each Council upgrade reaches level 500, with increasingly expensive purchases. Weapons and armor each add 2% of base army damage or health per level. Treasury begins with +12% income, then gives smaller gains at each later level. The Council shows your current bonus, next bonus, current level, and price. Free Folk can reach armor level 500 but pay 20% more for armor upgrades.

      A distinct army for every house

      Every house has its own specialist troop, production building, castle artwork, marching rules, and recruitment rules. Build your house structure with Shift+H. It automatically trains specialists and unlocks their paid companies with U, then Shift+5. Losing every matching structure locks paid recruitment again. Compare houses before starting to see each strategy. Rival houses follow the same rules.

      Menu shortcuts

      U opens Muster · B opens Build · C opens Council.
      Shift + number recruits companies or activates Council items. ⇧ means Shift. Building shortcuts use letters and work from any panel.
      Muster: 1 swordsmen, 2 archers, 3 cavalry, 4 catapults, 5 house specialist, 6 additional house regiment for eligible houses.
      Build: Shift+B barracks, Shift+A archery range, Shift+S stables, Shift+T watchtower, Shift+W siege works, Shift+H original house production building, Shift+J additional house facility for eligible houses. Then click your land; Esc or right-click cancels.
      Council: 1 house power, 2 weapons, 3 armor, 4 economy, 5 house guide, 6 town hall upgrade.
      Plain 1–9 recalls groups; Ctrl+1–9 assigns them. Ctrl+Shift+1–9 adds the selection to a group. Empty selection clears an assigned group. Menus work while paused. Each building icon shows its actual artwork.

      Reclaim the ruined castles

      Standard maps contain 3 neutral ruins, Epic maps 5 and Continent maps 6. Hold a ruin with 90% local troop dominance for 60 seconds, using the same contested rules as flags. Empty or contested rings freeze saved progress. The restored castle belongs to your house, automatically recruits defenders and immediately claims passable land within 8 tiles. Each castle adds 60 troop capacity. Enemy attacks can destroy it, removing its bonus and turning it into a recapturable ruin.

      Siege engines and rare regiments

      Bolton and Hightower can build trebuchet yards. Their powerful long-range trebuchets need 15 stationary seconds to deploy and fire slowly. Moving resets setup. Free Folk can build a Giant Camp to slowly train high-health giants whose club strikes splash nearby enemies. Tully can build a Sharpshooter Lodge for long-range, slow-firing marksmen. Build the new house facility with Shift+J and recruit with U, Shift+6. Each has its own painted sprite sheet.

      Fight for the flags

      Every map has marked banner objectives. Keep your faction, or your alliance in team modes, at 90% or more of all living troops inside the ring for 30 seconds. Your saved time freezes when the flag is empty or contested; reassert dominance to continue. Each capture rewards 8 Royal Guard, split between living allies in team modes. They have 50% more health and 35% more damage than ordinary house specialists and may temporarily exceed supply. After a capture, the flag rests for 20 seconds before reopening. Click an objective in the sidebar to locate it. Bannerfall awards victory at 5 captures; the other modes use flags for reinforcements.

      Upgrade and defend your capital

      Open Council to upgrade your town hall through 3 levels. Each level has a larger appearance, more maximum health and stronger defenses. Upgrading preserves its current health percentage. Every standing castle grants +60 troop capacity. Your faction survives while at least one castle remains. When a castle falls it becomes a neutral ruin; only losing your last castle eliminates your faction.

      Build a stronger watch

      Click one of your watchtowers to see its health, firepower, range and next upgrade. Upgrade it through Watchtower, Guard Tower and Royal Bastion for 250, then 450 gold. Health rises from 1,150 to 2,000 to 3,200; each tier also shoots farther and hits harder. The tower preserves its remaining health percentage when upgraded. Only your own towers can be upgraded.

      Command an alliance

      Team modes pair your house with an allied bot. Allied houses share a team color, cannot damage or capture from each other, and combine their flag presence and victory progress. Call assault, then click a target: allied bots gather for eight seconds and launch together. Call defense asks them to reinforce a position immediately. Bots also help defend allied capitals automatically. Your soldiers remain under your direct control; a team call directs your bot allies.

      Rivals make plans

      Rival commanders use massed assaults, cavalry raids, siege pushes, counterattacks and objective control. They gather forces, choose vulnerable targets and regroup between invasions. The house roster shows their current plan. Difficulty changes how quickly they assess and react.

      New house regiments

      Stark, Lannister, Targaryen and Tyrell can build a dedicated shield hall to produce Shield Bearers. Their huge shields block most frontal missile and melee damage, but their rear remains exposed. Mormont, Martell and Arryn can build their own lance academy to produce Lance Champions slowly. A long lance delivers a powerful first charge and heavy knockback, followed by much weaker, slow attacks; braced Frey/Bolton spears still stop a frontal charge. Every new house regiment has its own armor, colors and eight-frame sprite sheet, plus a distinct production building. Build with Shift+J, recruit with U, Shift+6. Training respects supply and individual rally points. River Rangers, Free Folk Berserkers and Ironborn Reavers now move 10% faster.

      Use sightlines and cover

      Mountains block arrows, bolts, watchtowers and keep fire. Direct-fire troops cannot shoot through a mountain corner; mountain passes stay open. Troops in forests take 25% less arrow and bolt damage. Archers on hills gain 0.6 tiles of range. Arcing catapult rocks can pass over mountains and ignore woodland cover. These rules apply to every house.

      Face the enemy

      Right-drag from the destination toward your intended front. The preview shows the line and facing arrow before you release. Move & face orders never chase enemies and become Hold on arrival. Troops turn to the chosen facing and keep it during the march. Backing away is 55% of forward speed; sideways movement is 80%. Use this to withdraw a line while keeping its weapons toward the pursuers. Ordinary orders release the chosen facing. Frontal infantry protection gives way to flank and rear attacks.

      Make cavalry hit hard

      Give riders a Conquer or Attack order with roughly three clear tiles for a straight run-up. Charges hit hard, scatter enemies and break through multiple ranks. Thorn Lancers hit and throw troops even harder. Bolton Dread Pikes and Frey Bridge Wardens can stop a frontal charge after holding still for half a second. Their spears face one direction: flank or circle behind them. Rear charge impacts deal 45% more damage and lose 55% less momentum; flank impacts gain 15% damage and lose 25% less momentum. A real run-up is still required. Pull horses out and regroup before another charge.

      Know your counters

      Braced spearmen stop frontal cavalry charges; ordinary swordsmen can be driven back. Cavalry breaks archers caught in the open. Frey Bridge Wardens and Bolton Dread Pikes are the dedicated spear and pike specialists. Other houses can deny riders a clear run-up, use forests and fortifications, or draw them into missile fire and surround them after a charge. Archers wear down infantry; siege engines tear down keeps. Catapult blasts scatter troops, so spread out against artillery.

      Conquest: destroy every enemy castle. Bannerfall: reach 5 flag captures or eliminate your enemies. Dominion: hold 65% of the realm after the opening 90 seconds or eliminate your enemies. Team versions combine alliance progress. Your faction is defeated only when its last castle falls. House advantages and powers apply equally to player and rival houses.
