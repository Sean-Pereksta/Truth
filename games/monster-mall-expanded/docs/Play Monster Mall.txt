MONSTER MALL — AFTER HOURS / EXPANDED EDITION

Open "Monster Mall - After Hours.html" in Chrome or Edge. Wait for the
characters to load, choose your difficulty, and select ENTER THE MALL.
The game is self-contained and works offline. No installation is needed.

EVERY NIGHT
Find three emergency fuses: Hollow Records, the east service stockroom,
and the upper west gallery. Northern stairs reach Level 02. Install the
fuses at security at the far north end. Complete ALL tasks for your chosen
difficulty, then use the main doors at the south end to escape.
Power alone does not unlock the exit. The checklist shows what remains;
amber map markers show fuses and tasks. Muted blue markers are on another floor.

CHOOSE YOUR NIGHT
Easy: two beasts, slower reactions, shorter memories, five starting flashes.
  Added task: recover the SECURITY KEYCARD in Night Owl Books (east, Level 01).
Normal: three beasts that listen and search last-known positions.
  All Easy tasks, plus close the COOLANT VALVE in west maintenance
  (northwest staff corridor).
Hard: monsters run 15% faster than Normal, with better perception and anticipation.
  All Normal tasks, plus reset the GALLERY RELAY on the upper east gallery,
  near the south end. The relay needs restored power.
Nightmare: monsters run 32% faster than Normal, share sightings and flank.
  All Hard tasks, plus find the SHUTTER CRANK in west deliveries (southwest
  staff corridor), then use it on the mechanism beside the main exit.
  Restore power before winding the shutter.
Insane: monsters run 50% faster than Normal, with coordinated hunters and
  a fourth creature, THE STILL ONE.
  All Nightmare tasks, plus cancel LOCKDOWN at the north security terminal
  after power and every other task are complete. The override makes noise.

THE STILL ONE — INSANE ONLY
An abnormally tall, dark figure with a pale mask and long hanging hands.
It stops when you keep it in view with an unobstructed sightline.
Turn away and it can move again. Walls and stairs still constrain it.
It can be stunned. Do not mistake stillness for safety.

CONTROLLER (STANDARD XBOX / PLAYSTATION LAYOUT)
Connect by USB or Bluetooth, focus the game window, and press a button.
Left stick — Move (analog, with drift dead zone)
Right stick — Look
RB / R1 or left stick click — Hold to sprint
A / Cross — Interact / select
X / Square — Stun flash
B / Circle — Toggle crouch / back in menus
Y / Triangle — Flashlight
View / Create / Share — Map
Menu / Options — Pause / resume
D-pad or left stick — Navigate menus
Left / right — Change difficulty or the selected setting
A / Cross — Activate focused menu item
B / Circle — Close settings or go back
Disconnecting a controller during play pauses the game. Release held controls
after switching menus, reconnecting, or returning to the game window.
Controllers must be exposed by the browser with a compatible standard layout;
generic devices with unusual mappings may need their manufacturer's driver.
If sound is silent, click or press a keyboard key once to enable audio, then
continue with the controller. Browser fullscreen can require a mouse click.

KEYBOARD / MOUSE
WASD — Move
Mouse — Look (drag if pointer capture is unavailable)
Arrow keys — Alternative look
Shift — Sprint
C — Toggle crouch
E — Interact / collect
Q — Stun flash
F — Flashlight
M — Floor map
O — Controls & settings
Esc or P — Pause
F11 — Fullscreen (also available using the fullscreen buttons)
Touch: left thumb moves, drag the right side to look, use action buttons.

SURVIVAL
One capture is fatal. Save a stun flash for close encounters. A flash also
frees a shopper being held. Refills are hidden around the mall.
Beasts grow after feeding up to a fixed, stair-safe size. Every creature stays
below the lowest shop doorway and ceiling; The Still One has its own smaller
scale cap. Walking is quieter and conserves stamina.
Crouch with the light off behind cover to break pursuit.
Hunters remember observed positions; louder movement and machinery give
them new clues. Higher difficulties react faster and search longer. Monsters spread their patrols
across shops, staff passages and both galleries. After losing a target, they
check different areas instead of always following each other.

SHOPPERS
Frightened shoppers look for counters, shelving and other solid cover. They
run to a protected position, crouch low, and huddle quietly while danger passes.
A monster that sees or reaches them can still find them. Hiding shoppers wait
for a safe moment to move, then head for the exit once every lock is released.

SETTINGS & SAVES
Sensitivity, movement, field of view, brightness, quality, volume, invert look,
toggle sprint, head motion, camera shake, sound captions, and gentle effects.
The difficulty selection and preferences save on this device when browser
storage is available. Best escape times are recorded separately per difficulty.
Gentle effects reduces flashes, flicker, camera shake, and head motion.

EDIT / REBUILD
Editable files are in Source. Run "node build.mjs" there to rebuild the HTML
one folder above. Node.js is only needed for rebuilding, never for playing.
All art, characters, sound synthesis and engine code are bundled offline.
Models contains the original reusable GLB assets. The new Still One is
generated by the game's creature rig; the original GLB is preserved.
