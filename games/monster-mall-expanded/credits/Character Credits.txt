Changes made for this game:
- Embedded original model, photographic maps, full skeleton and facial morphs for offline play.
- Converted surface shaders to standard PBR materials for the mall's moving lights.
- Added three restrained garment-color variants while preserving the photographed face and hair.
- Added articulated idle, walk, panic-running and grab-struggle movements from the original bind pose.
- Added small blink and open-mouth expression animation using the original facial morphs.
- Normalized the contained character to approximately 1.85 meters.
