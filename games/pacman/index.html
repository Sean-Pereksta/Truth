FIELD MANUAL

Command the maze

01

Choose your team

Pac: choose Gold, Jade, or Coral. Two AI partners share your vision and pellet progress. Each team owns its army, Spark, and Power. Eat every pellet OR destroy the Ghost Citadel together.

Ghosts: command a powerful legion from the central Citadel. Hunt troops for Dread. Eliminate all three Pac-Men to win. A commander's death eliminates his team; surviving partners continue.

02

Give orders

Left click selects. Drag a box for groups; touching a troop with the box includes it. Shift adds units. Right click commands. A attacks each troop’s nearest visible enemy.

Ctrl + 1–9 assigns selected troops or structures. Shift + 1–9 adds to a group. Press its number to recall; press twice to focus. Click the group badges for the same controls. Mac: use Command instead of Ctrl.

T opens troops; B opens buildings. Z / C / V / N / G activates the labeled option in the open tab. E always activates Power; Q casts a Ghost Binder snare. Mouse edges, arrows or W/S/D pan; wheel zooms.

03

Fight through fog

All three Pac teams share live vision and explored ground. Every pellet grants ghosts a little local vision; cleared routes and outer lands need ghost scouts. Dim pellet markers are unconfirmed.

Binders cast an area snare. Watch its ground marker and move out before it lands. Ghost players can select Binders, press Q, and target the ground.

04

Build your foothold

Settle the outer belt and use its ruined hiding places to break enemy sight. Command Nest: main recruitment. Refuge: forward recruitment and vision. Pulse Tower: defense. Digestor: Spark and Power. Sanctuary: heals Pac-Man and allied troops within 6 tiles after 4 seconds without taking damage; walls block healing.

Ghost Lairs recruit, Watch Relays reveal a wide area, and Spectral Spires defend. Click a top-left troop box to cancel and refund it. Buildings fit on scouted, clear, unoccupied ground.

Easy gives Hard AI partners and Easy opponents. Medium makes both equal. Hard gives Easy partners and Hard opponents. Nightmare keeps Easy partners against Nightmare foes.
