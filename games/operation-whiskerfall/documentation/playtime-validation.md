# First-clear and optional playtime

The first-clear target is 10–14 hours, followed by 5–8 additional hours of authored optional play. Human active-play time remains unmeasured for both. The browser progression checks use supplied prerequisites and reposition the party. A complete simulated boss fight measures that encounter under a specific loadout. Neither gives a campaign duration. [Acceptance report](acceptance-report.md) · [Combat measurements](combat-playtest.md)

## What a fresh campaign contains

| Progression | Verified in the shipped build |
| --- | --- |
| Investigation | The three acts contain 3, 6 and 3 reports. Six leads are on the surface and six are in distinct dungeons. Every report offers observation or contested recovery. The player files one source per report. All eight regional commanders expose a later lead. The final vault hatch opens the courtyard checkpoint before the Whisker fight. |
| Assignment book | 159 assignments comprise 25 marked main and 134 marked optional. The main book covers 16 outposts, 8 regional commanders and the Berlin finale. Commander and finale victories also serve the investigation. Counting those fights twice would inflate the estimate. |
| Debrief and forge | Each first filing grants 160 gold, 12 scrap and 1 core to each active or resting hero. All twelve filings grant 1,920 gold, 144 scrap and 12 cores per eligible hero. One filing funds the newly available Lockjaw Scattergun or Latch Hammer at 75 gold and 6 scrap apiece. The 20 new equipment patterns span 0–10 confirmed clues. |
| Optional objectives | The optional book includes 20 guardian-cavern assignments, four of which are the named Rimeglass, Cindercoil, Zephyr and Kraken expeditions. Another 25 assignments specify 60 scripted steps. Three post-credits dossier exercises revisit existing interiors with distinct conditions and 2, 2 and 3 marked objectives before extraction. Practice wins and repeat scores are recorded separately. |

The opening observation point is 336 world units from the starting coordinates in a straight line. The alternative satchel is 1,285 units away. The four new connecting roads measure 5,023, 4,810, 4,810 and 4,056 units along their exported points. An uninterrupted observation takes four seconds once the player reaches the point. The eight optional courier routes each measure 917 units, and the eight regional defense assignments specify 45 seconds on station. These figures describe particular activities. Travel choices, combat, exploring, reading, shopping, retries and player familiarity still determine how long a session takes. ## Human measurement plan

1. Enlist first-time players for three independent solo campaigns and three independent local co-op campaigns. Record the build, party, device, difficulty or assist settings and prior twin-stick experience. Start each on an empty slot. Players may follow the journal, use normal travel and take detours. Scrap farming and repeated practice are never assigned to fill a time target.
2. Start a stopwatch at New Campaign confirmation. Stop the first-clear clock when the credits begin. Include navigation, reading, menu decisions, fights, deaths and retreads. Pause only for breaks away from the game. Record time at each report, commander, first forge purchase, courtyard and credits. Mark intentional optional detours separately. Preserve the save and split log across sessions.
3. From that same save, separately time first completions of the four named expeditions and their turn-ins, the three hidden assignments (The Gunsmith Below, The Last Watch and The Blackglass Trail), and the three dossier exercises through extraction. Let the party choose the order. If it already finished a listed objective before credits, record its earlier split once, alongside the smaller amount of additional content left after credits. Log any further unique optional assignments by ID and duration. Keep repeat mastery attempts in their own column.
4. Report individual times and solo/co-op medians and ranges for title-to-credits, optional detours before credits and additional first completions after credits. Include unfinished runs, where players stalled, and the share of time spent retracing routes or repeating fights. Compare each observed group with the two targets. If the post-credits sample runs short, list the completed activities and time left unaccounted for. Preserve the raw splits for the next revision.
