All 159 original quests passed their individual Chrome fixtures. The exported stable IDs and ordering matched the live catalogue. Ironveil opens after the Cinder Rail Depot report and Blackglass after Berlin clearance. Both optional investigations keep independent completion records.

Each original assignment below was accepted through its contact’s dialogue, advanced to its objective, reported at its designated contact, paid once and reopened through the title screen’s Continue action in Chrome. For the 158 assignments with a new field choice, both physical evidence sites were visited in independent saves. Each test restored the pending file, the confirmed choice, the ready turn-in and the paid result. The Berlin finale used a migrated campaign with earned clearance.

Fixtures supplied prerequisites, expedition keys and materials, moved heroes to the locations, called the game’s enemy-defeat handlers and advanced encounter clocks. Escort transit was accelerated. The tests operated chamber mechanisms, cleared trial waves, defeated guardians and claimed their actual reliquaries. These results cover quest logic, save handling and payouts. Player timing, strength of deductions and physical controller usability need human playtesting.

**Save behavior.** M1 retains pre-upgrade accepted assignments on their original objective and preserves partial stages, ready turn-ins and completed grants. Newly accepted field files save their chosen source and reward only through the original completion transaction. M2 also restores previously earned Berlin access through the existing archive migration. Legacy records use the same IDs and numeric indices.

**Campaign chronology.** A separate 225-check Chrome run accepted and reported the 24 regional story assignments in order, defeated the eight commanders, filed all twelve dossier operations and opened the Berlin courtyard. The final confrontation led to the epilogue, credits, Dawnbridge, the original quest giver’s one-time reward, three optional exercises and the practice commander. A two-controller fixture then used staged late-game clearance to follow the credits back to Dawnbridge, take the night tram, accept the optional Blackglass case and return both heroes to town.

## Reproduce the checks

Run `node tests/field-assignments.cjs` with Playwright available and CHROME_PATH set to the browser executable. FIELD_IDS accepts a regular expression for a single quest or family. FIELD_RESULT optionally records the row-level results in JSON. Run `node tests/city-investigations.cjs` for the optional city cases and `node tests/recon-campaign.cjs` for the twelve canonical operations.
