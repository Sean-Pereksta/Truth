Distant vaults and guarded keys

Four optional dungeons append to the sixteen existing caves. Existing cave IDs,
quests and saved progress keep their original meaning. Their approaches and
collision maps differ; the established guardian chamber bounds remain shared
so the existing readable boss attacks keep their tested space.

Rimeglass Reservoir — Frostmarch, recommended level 12
Quest: A Breath Beneath the Ice. Contact: Keeper Thistle.
Two lake loops form a figure eight around a permanently safe stone crossing.
Activate the west and east pumps in either order, then the central regulator.
The Rimeglass Valve Key is in the guarded Frozen Courier Cache in Stonecrest,
east of Badger's Lantern. This source is outside the target dungeon.
Reward after returning the seal to Thistle: Winter's Whisper and Frost Breath.

Cindercoil Foundry — Emberworks, recommended level 19
Quest: The Furnace That Wouldn't Die. Contact: Foreman Brindle.
Separate valve wings flank a furnace. Turning both valves cools the cross-bridge
and opens the guardian gate. Each wing can be visited first; no puzzle timer.
The Cindercoil Foundry Key is in the guarded Smuggler's Tool Chest in Reedwater,
east of Reedlight Refuge. This source is outside the target dungeon.
Reward after returning the seal to Brindle: Ember Bastion Armor.

Zephyr Reliquary — Reedwater, recommended level 16
Quest: The Wind Remembers. Contact: Pilgrim Sora.
Offset terraces form a loop around a central sanctuary. Ring three terrace
bells in any order, then activate the sanctuary. No entrance key is required.
Reward after returning the seal to Sora: Galeweave Mantle and Aurora Well.

The Drowned Maw — Sunken Crown Isles, recommended level 26
Quest: The Beast Beneath the Crown. Contact: Diver Pearl.
Flooded piers and three tide anchors lead to Nacremaw, the Great Kraken.
No entrance key or overall kill timer. The Kraken is a separate three-phase
boss with tentacle lanes, delayed slams, warned ink fans and gapped undertow
rings. Its behavior and illustrated rendering live in js/core/kraken.js.
Reward after returning the seal to Pearl: Legendary Sea Beast Armor, with
spectral tentacles from repeated firearm hits and slowing ink left by dodging.

Shared keys and rewards

Each key cache creates a bounded three-enemy patrol when investigated. Defeat
all three, then interact again to collect the key. There is no kill timer and
no random key drop. Both heroes use the same persistent, nonconsumable keyring.
Normal entry and active-dungeon restoration both require the correct key.
NPC clues and locked entrance labels name the source. Accepting a quest with a
missing key tracks its source cache instead of the locked cave.

The reliquary records an expedition seal. It gives no random equipment choice
or extra generic treasure payout. The named quest contact must receive the
seal through Finish quest; completedQuests then drives the separate equipment
and ability reward modules. Repeated claims cannot duplicate a seal or key.

Prism Sentry

An appended slate-blue armored mouse with a cyan prism backpack. Two parallel
frost lanes commit to their positions for a 1.15-second visible warning. Their
middle gap is safe. Walls clip the lanes. One volley can hit a hero once and
causes at most 0.65 seconds of slow after actual damage. An ordinary dodge
blocks both damage and slow. Stun, freeze, death, travel and lost warnings
cancel a pending attack. Both warnings must allocate before an attack starts.
Its 1.4-second recovery is stationary and takes 15% extra incoming damage.

Save and integration details

Data: data/distant-vaults.js, loaded after dungeons, coastal content and deep
enemy definitions. Surface site installation is idempotent and available as
Content.installDistantVaultSites(World) for offline map tooling. Runtime:
js/world/distant-vaults.js after dungeon/checkpoint/turn-in/guardian extensions,
then js/core/prism-sentry.js and js/core/kraken.js. Illustrated expedition art
loads afterward. The only legacy dungeon edit skips its old mural HUD for
these custom layouts.

dungeonStates remains keyed by cave ID. Dense boolean vaultNodes retain partial
mechanisms; solved gates remain open. Guard IDs are stable. Saved guard health
is restored exactly and pending attacks are cleared. Leaving and re-entering
does not heal a living guardian. A full-party checkpoint rescue still resets
the current fight under the game's established rules while retaining keys and
solved mechanisms. Guard damage tuning can be reconstructed from stable IDs
after checkpoint reset strips transient fields.

Game.distantVaultKeys is a Set in memory and an array in snapshots.
Game.distantVaultKeyRuns stores each key patrol's three enemy records. This
preserves injured guards across streaming and reload without spawning copies.
The Kraken remembers its first-encounter party size in state.krakenPartySize;
switching co-op does not multiply its health repeatedly.

Automated verification

tests/distant-vaults.test.cjs exercises real generated terrain, flood-fill
reachability of all mechanisms, locked/open collision gates, distinct layout
signatures, source caches, actual guard kills, repeated save/load, injured
enemy health, streaming recovery, shared keys, checkpoint rescue, partial
any-order mechanisms and the real NPC turn-in flow.
tests/prism-sentry.test.cjs exercises actual damage and effects: timing,
committed targeting, safe gap, dodge, interrupts, streaming loss, co-op,
effect-pool pressure, wall clipping and saved attack cleanup.
tests/kraken.test.cjs exercises all five attacks and three phases, actual hits,
gap/dodge avoidance, bounded ink, warning allocation, recovery damage,
save/party invariance, persistent death/seal, and exact Sea Beast quest grants.
tools/kraken-balance.cjs uses real inputs, bullets and AI with ordinary level26
equipment. Its controller reads visible attack records perfectly; it is
repeatable mechanics evidence, not a human playtest or a difficulty guarantee.

The first balance pass defeated the Kraken in 17.0 seconds solo and 12.4
seconds in co-op. Its final tuning uses 48,000 health solo or 72,000 co-op,
190 base damage, an armored shell taking 20% damage outside recovery, and
125% damage while exposed. Warning durations remain unchanged. With the same
Reedrunner Carbine +4, ordinary Dawnkeeper Battle Vest, reinforcement +5 and
level26 skills, the repeatable controller won in 60.0 seconds solo and 46.0
seconds co-op. Both runs exercised all three phases and all five attacks.
The solo hero took 76 damage; the co-op team took 87 total damage. Neither
used its three starting medkits. Peak ink pressure was fourteen projectiles.
The before/after JSON reports preserve equipment and remaining ammunition.
These are successful automated avoidance runs, not claims that a human player
will find the fight easy or difficult.

Illustrated environments

Each new dungeon uses its own original opaque floor texture and transparent
eight-prop sheet. A separate painted wall atlas supplies an icy masonry wall,
hot copper-pipe wall, mossy ruin wall and barnacled tidal wall, with matching
corner stones. Source pixels are retained; measured crop metadata avoids
adjacent sprites and keeps props grounded.

js/world/distant-vault-scenery.js paints these textures over the exact union of
the authored floor rectangles. It calculates exposed union edges for walls,
including the guardian arena. Shared room seams and door openings receive no
wall. Walls and background props are drawn outward before opaque walkable
floors, so their decorative overhang cannot obscure an open route. Puzzle
devices use thematic painted props; high-contrast labels, explicit active states,
closed gates and combat warnings remain above the scenery. Sealed gates use
the matching wall artwork within their exact bounds; opening removes the
barrier completely. No collision or
combat data changes. At most two cached floor surfaces remain in memory; an
opened furnace bridge or replacement art invalidates the relevant cache.

tests/distant-vault-scenery.test.cjs checks source files, genuine PNG alpha
transparency and measured crops,
all four themes, exposed boundary normals, clear door openings, exact floor
clipping, drawing order, state invariance, cache bounds and missing-art fallback.
tools/render-distant-scenery.cjs produces PNG previews through the actual game
terrain renderer using native Canvas, without browser automation.
