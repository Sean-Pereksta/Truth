# Masterwork armor

Visit a resistance forge to craft these patterns. Each hero crafts and equips their own armor. Crafting grants the item once, equips it, and records a discovery celebration; later equipment changes are free from the inventory. All three use the ordinary armor slot and preserve the existing base outfit and reinforcement ranks.

| Set | Unlock and one-time cost | Fighting style |
|---|---|---|
| Stormrunner Harness | 2 commanders; 1,800 gold, 90 scrap, 4 cores | Dodging loads up to two rounds from reserve and gives the next shot +35% damage for three seconds. 20% armor and 45 health. |
| Bastion Bulwark | 4 commanders; 3,400 gold, 150 scrap, 7 cores | Walking is 14% slower. Stand still for 0.35 seconds to reduce frontal damage by another 30%; a guarded hit empowers the next melee swing by 30% for three seconds. Moving, dodging and swinging break the brace. 32% armor and 70 health. |
| Spellweaver Mantle | 6 commanders; 5,400 gold, 220 scrap, 10 cores | Successful abilities have 20% shorter cooldowns and grant a 2.5-second ward absorbing 35 + twice the hero's level damage, up to 75. Firearms deal 15% less damage. 18% armor and 40 health. |

Changing armor clears temporary charges. Wards replace rather than stack, and armor changes never restore lost health. Checkpoint recovery, travel and loading clear transient combat powers. Forge-only patterns cannot enter ordinary random cache or shop rolls.

Each outfit has eight original painted body animations for both orange and white cats. The armor follows running, dodging, injury and downed states while existing aiming, weapon arms and melee animation remain active.

New firearms and armor receive per-hero discovery receipts. A small field notice keeps combat moving; the larger celebration waits until danger and other menus have finished. Equip primary, equip backup or keep the item, using mouse, keyboard or controller. Pending discoveries survive saves and never pay an equipment reward a second time.
