# Dungeon approaches

The entrance and its field station have separate interaction areas. Approach the painted cave mouth to descend with both heroes. Follow the nearby trail marks to investigate an optional station. Earlier caves retain their guards, keys, original entrance position and expedition objectives. Their exterior carvings describe the local puzzle or survival trial.

Survey records use the existing shared world ledger. Both heroes can read them again. A defeated outer guard stays defeated across a rescue or reload, and the ordinary entrance remains available throughout the optional encounter.

The exterior gallery has six purpose-painted reconnaissance stations, eight regional cavern mouths and six ground paintings. Hushfen uses wet sedge, Glasswind uses heather and slate, and Gloamroot uses forest litter. Mirrored edges join the 640-pixel terrain chunks. Soft-edged forecourts blend each landmark into its surroundings. Atmospheric cues and trail markings animate on the ground. Entrance illustrations fade over a hero, and their original collision footprints remain in force.
