The first mouse squeak is a performed human imitation. The other mouse calls use pet-rat recordings as rodent character voices. The qubodup attack and pain sounds derive from Zabuhailo's `ratSqueak.wav`. The designed death effect also incorporates [Meat Grinder05M.wav](https://freesound.org/people/moneykube/sounds/38747/) by moneykube, a squeaky toy recording. Both underlying recordings are CC0.

Edits for the game: short excerpts, mono conversion where needed, low-frequency rumble filtering, anti-alias filtering, 22,050 Hz resampling, gentle fade-in/out, level balancing, and PCM 16-bit WAV export. Freesound clips were converted from the public high-quality MP3 previews; the original OpenGameArt WAV/FLAC recordings were used for the other clips. The purr excerpts have fades and are intended as occasional one-shot sounds, not seamless loops.

`sources.json` records each file's exact source, trim interval, duration, output format, levels, download link, and SHA-256 checksum. The game's embedded sound bank is generated from these same WAV files so the sounds remain available when the game is opened locally without a server.
